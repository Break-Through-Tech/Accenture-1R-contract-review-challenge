# Exploratory Data Analysis Summary

The CUAD training dataset contains 408 contracts. The data was checked for missing values and duplicate contract titles. No missing titles, paragraphs, contract text, or duplicate contract titles were found. The contract length was explored using both character and word counts. The median contract length was 35,960.5 characters, and the median word count was 5,357.5 words. Using the IQR method, 33 contract length outliers were identified. These outliers represent unusually long contracts and were not automatically removed because differences in contract length may be valid. The dataset contains 41 question categories. "Parties" was the most common category with 2,012 questions, while "Third Party Beneficiary" was the least common with 410 questions. This shows that the question categories are not equally represented in the dataset. There are 22,450 total questions. Of these, 11,180 have answers and 11,270 do not have answers, making the answered and unanswered questions nearly balanced. Contract length, word count, question category, and answer presence were explored as potential features that may be useful for the contract review task.
